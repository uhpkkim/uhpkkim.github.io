GREETINGS FROM...

      GREETINGS FROM... is/was/is a blurry experimental play about postcards and airplanes created by the Are You Thinking What I'm Thinking? Collective.

Amelia Airplane (played collectively by the entire ensemble) crashes in an unknown place and must pull herself back together with the help of the postcards she carries, the stories they tell and the lost messages they hold that will now be undelivered. Along the way she may or may not have to wrestle with her own cosmic fate.

Facilitated by Theresa Columbus, Luu Pham, and Jakey Zabawa

Devised and performed by Rowan Gardner, Dave Iden, PK Kim, Francesco Leandri, Luu Pham, Joanna Plasencia, and Jakey Zabawa

Greetings From... was developed and performed as a double-feature in the summer of 2024, alongside its sister play Well Ends Well That's All (but I wasn't in that one).
