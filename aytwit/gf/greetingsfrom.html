GREETINGS FROM...


GREETINGS FROM... is/was/is a blurry experimental play about postcards and airplanes produced by the Are You Thinking What I'm Thinking? Collective. It was devised by Theresa Columbus, Luu Pham, Jakey Zabawa, Rowan Gardner, Dave Iden, PK Kim, Francesco Leandri, and Joanna Plasencia.

Amelia Airplane (played collectively by the entire ensemble) crashes in an unknown place and must pull herself back together with the help of the postcards she carries, the stories they tell and the lost messages they hold that will now be undelivered. Along the way she may or may not have to wrestle with her own cosmic fate.

Greetings From... was developed and performed as a double-feature in the summer of 2024, alongside its sister play Well Ends Well That's All (but I wasn't in that one).

Material from Greetings From.. was also used as the foundation for several short performances at various variety shows throughout 2024. Photos on this page were taken by Theresa Columbus, Francisco Benavides, Caitlin Weaver, and JB Aris.
