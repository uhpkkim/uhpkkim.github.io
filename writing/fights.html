A Hot Girl Kept Feeding Me Boigahs
Until I Got Sleepy

a hot girl kept feeding me boigahs until I got sleepy
then she kept trying to make out with me
while I was falling asleep.
she said she likes it when the girls she makes out with
are full of boigahs.

I don't know how true that is, but they were bogo.
they were buy one, get one free.
the hot girl bought em all
and then she got all the rest free.

I finally fell asleep and dreamed about some mummies
trying to rob a bank.
I woke back up and the hot girl was still
trying to make out with me.

as we were making out, she was on her phone.
she was dialing 1-800-BOGO-BOIGAHS.

I fell back asleep again
and she entered my dream through my mouth.
I was dreaming that I was falling asleep
at my job at the bank.
she came in and kept trying to make out with me
at my job at the bank,
but I was worried about making out with someone
while on the clock.

I didn't wanna get fired because
I needed the money to get new tires
for my bicycle that I had used
to bike to her apartment back in the awake world.
(I've been saving up for a bicycle that can ride itself)

she says I should take all the trash from my job home
so she could use it for an abstract sculpture
that she's been working on.

I asked her to explain the abstract sculpture
using only words and sentences
but before she could get to
the third vague shape description,
the mummies finally showed up to rob the bank
and then I woke up again.

when I woke back up,
there was this hot girl making out with me.
I told her I had to bike home, so that I could
dream on my own mattress by myself.

she reminded me that
my bicycle had two flat tires
from when we were bicycling
down the jones falls
and we ran over a bag of broken glass
that an old polish lady had dropped in the road
because she had gotten startled
because she thought she had seen
the apparition of her long lost lover
stepping out of a toyota corolla...

the hot girl told me she would call me an uber.
I told her I was allergic to yams
but then she said uber again
and then I understood what word she said.

the uber driver took
the most imaginary route to my destination.
it was like he did the whole thing upside down.
on the car radio, I heard a news report:
apparently interpol is hunting down a hot girl
because her abstract sculptures
contain too many illegal vibes.

when I got home and exited the uber,
I accidentally left all of my leftover boigahs
still sitting in the back seat.

I tried to call uber to retrieve my lost luggage,
but the driver had apparently disappeared
immidiately after he drove away from my house
and nobody ever heard from him ever again...

the next morning,
I felt so bloated from the night before.
on the phone, the hot girl says
she can put me in touch with a boigah doula.


valuables

I keep all of my most expensive and valuable items
in front of the front door to my apartment

this way, if any burglars were to rob me,
they don't need to rummage through my entire house

they don't have to pull out all my dresser drawers
they don't have to go through my whole closet
they don't have to pull all the books off the shelf
looking for some kind of secret compartment
in and out in five minutes or less

  many

   many

    thieves
     enter my apartment weekly
     and, upon the realization that there will be
     no challenge or mystery to be found here,
     simply turn around
                                                   and leave


my son (a graph)

the subaru is only parked two blocks away, but
my son doesn't want to walk an extra block and a half

this orange line represents how far we have to walk

the blue line represents how long my son is willing to
listen to me talk before he stops paying attention

my son declares that if he could just turn into a wheel
(which is his life's dream) that he could finally just
roll the two blocks to the car

but then... he wouldn't need to use the car then right?
because he'll be a wheel?? but no no he says
that's not the point and that i don't understand him

i guess it's a generational thing?
times were simply different when i was growing up...

my son's favorite hobby is watching a cooking show
hosted by people who decided to become pop stars
a week ago and already have a record deal lined up

i love my son but he's the worst

right now he's just sitting around watching this show,
but one day he will be a big, admirable wheel who will
bring a great positive change to this world we live in

the purple line represents how long it will take him to
learn how to do his own dang laundry for once

the green line represents how long it will take him to
bring a great positive change to this world we live in

i go get the car and loop around the block
i'm double-parked out front

the other cars honking remind me that
when he's my age, cars won't exist anymore
so i can't be that mad at him

Symbiosis

There's a fungus growing on my foot and I worry
that it will soon be more me than I am.

I frolicked outside with a wet sock for too long and
now I've got a foot fungus that I'm worried will grow
so large that it will be more me than me.

Once the fungus grows large enough, it will become
the basis for a small religion. Many will call this
a "cult", but I would just call it a small religion.

I myself won't be a member of this religion, but
I will stand by their right to base an entire spiritual
movement entirely around my foot if it helps them
find community and belonging.

It's not really about my foot, exactly.
It's more about the fungus, which is an entirely
different entity altogether really. It just happens to
live on my foot. My foot is its house.

NOTE TO SELF:
maybe change "foot" in the story to "hand"
or something. I just feel like that might work better
and make things easier, logistically

The church of my hand fungus convenes on mondays.

At first, I will resist having a gaggle of people
following me around everywhere once a week.

I will try to go about my errands and goings-on, but
eventually I will realize it's simply easier to schedule
mondays as the day I have a congregation conducting
ceremonies and special practices in my vicinity.

I will have to lie to my boss about why I need off
on mondays now, but she will already have been
suspicious, considering how many of these people
had been showing up to the store unannounced
just to follow me around once a week and
set up little prayer circles in the fitting rooms,
set up little altars on the jewelry display cases.

They won't actually care about me, my personality,
or my involvement. I will only appear in passing
during a few passages in their scripture. I will be
more like a small cameo, a necessary plot device
that gets brushed over once it's served its purpose.

They'll probably discuss whether it would be best to
just kill me and/or attempt to extract the fungus, but
I assume they'll decide the risk to the fungus is too high.

I'll finally get fed up with all of this years later,
when the leader of the church eventually tells me to
clear my schedule for Saturday the 23rd so that
I can attend a "cult fair."

It's like a job fair or a career fair, but for cults
looking to recruit new members. Everyone's got their
little booths and tables set up and everything.

(Huh. I guess maybe they are just a cult?)

Me and the leader will get into a whole fight
because I was planning to go to my crush's
specifically-themed birthday party that same day.
And things will be going very well with this crush
so I will definitely want to show up!

(The party theme will be that we all have to dress up
like our favorite Keanu Reeves movie. It's not
that you dress up like his character in the movie,
you have to dress up like the movie itself.)

That's when I'll finally break ties with the cult and
change addresses. This schizm will end up escalating
the cult into a doomsday cult, which felt inevitable.
I'll end up spending years in therapy unpacking how
I feel guilty and responsible for all the deaths, etc.

But anyway none of that has happened yet.
That's still a few years away.

For now, the fungus just itches real bad.

But it's annoyingly ironic because the fungus
is growing on my scratching hand!

How do you scratch the hand that scratches??
Who scratches the scratchers?

NOTE TO SELF:
maybe change "fungus" to something a little
less gross so people don't get all grossed out.

The leech that has attached itself to my hand and
refuses to let go doesn't say much but I think
I've become endeared to him.

My boss thinks that I should do something about it
because it might freak out the customers but I just
tell customers that he's my emotional support animal.

It is becoming a problem, though,
that he keeps drooling a bit and
I keep getting blood on the merchandise
and the POS system...

NOTE TO SELF
ok maybe that's worse?
maybe we should just switch back to the fungus.

A customer came in today who was very fascinated
by the fungus on my hand and asked if she could get
a closer look. She grabbed my arm and pulled out
a magnifying glass and inspected.

NOTE TO SELF
oh no... ok, so now I miss the leech. I kinda liked him.
hm, ok. I think I've got it.

The curious customer looked up from her
magnifying glass and said that she's never seen
a river leech and a robust fungal infection living
in such happy symbiosis on the same hand before!
Wow! Someone should write a book about it!

Yeah, you know, we have our struggles,
but we get along pretty well. Like a little family.

She said she wants to tell some of her pals about it.
She knows some people who would be very interested.

What, are they, like... in a little club or something?
That's cute! No one does clubs anymore.

A really really cool song plays
when you're running across the tundra.

You can't hear it though.
But everyone else can when they watch you run.

The bassline expecially, is like, really good.
But I imagine most people's favorite part would be
when the 90's jungle breakbeat kicks in.

The people searching for you can hear it, too.
But their version is slightly different because
it's kind of more about them than you.

A really beautiful, heartwrenching song plays
when your mom worries about you and wonders
what you're up to whenever you don't call.

The lighting and camera angles are really
well-crafted when you finally manage to find
warmth and shelter from your ordeals,
but you don't notice them
because you're preoccupied, obviously.

No music plays and the lighting is bad
when I stand alone in my bathroom
and can't figure out what to do next.

A really clever narrative metaphor occurs
when our fight scene reaches its climax
and I realize that I was wrong --
it's not that you think I'm annoying because
you're projecting your feelings onto me!
But rather, I'm just actually annoying.

A catchy little jingle plays whenever you finally
find the strength to overcome your obstacles.

Sometimes, the really cool song still plays
in my headphones when I'm cooking,
even though we haven't spoken in several years.

I'm Gonna Fight My Enemy This Time

I'm gonna do it.
  She shows up everywhere I am
  and slaps everyone in the face
  with her dirty, filthy hair.

I'm gonna do it I'm gonna go fight her.
  She uses her hypnosis powers to lead
  otherwise-reasonable dykes astray
  from their goals and values.

Ugh, there she is!
  The feds paid her to hit on my ex
  and sneeze on my roommates.

I'm about to go fight her this time.
I keep saying it, but I mean it this time.


* My enemy doesn't know who I am,
   or that she is my enemy.
