DIRT // DRIFT // SIFT

This collection is an ongoing project built off of my previous Crunch Font experiments. Several works from the original Crunch Font studies (and mental illness) lead me down a path towards obsessing over this method/structure. Almost all of the work here uses brand new fonts I created that are not available to the public.

Unfortunately, I suffered a disasterous harddrive failure about a month after making many of these, and I lost several entire pieces, ALL working files, and ALL of the new fonts I crafted to make these. The things here are what I was able to recover, and I'm slowly working on restoring some lost peices based on grainy photos of my laptop monitor lol.
