WHO IS PK?
PK (she/her) is a multi/interdisciplinary artist attempting to work in as many art mediums as humanly possible. Hisorically her main focuses have been MUSIC and ILLUSTRATION, but she has also worked in: sculpture, animation, film, theater, performance art, writing, video-game design, and live comedy. She also coded this website.

PK is also:
- disabled and neurodivergent, transgender and queer, white, and a full grown old adult
- hoarder of esoteric wizardry, champion of gimmicks
- defender of dirt, the clunky blurry and outdated
- facilitator of collaboration
- devout hater of capitalism
- local karaoke life coach
- pretty okay at yo-yo
